# resolution

**Short:** The mesh, not the chart. The scale at which a claim was checked. Changing it is lossy, and "survives G" without it is incomplete.
**From:** Hidden in `vary_floors.py`'s `_z()` helper (round to 6 decimals before comparing verdicts) from the start; first turned as a knob in `vary_lenses.py` L5; named as a separate kind of operation in `vary_gauges.py` G3–G5 after Kelly asked about "true/real at different resolutions." The vocabulary is physics' renormalisation group (Wilson 1971).
**Why it is needed:** The series said "this claim survives every floor" for three scripts before noticing that every verdict had been rounded first. A floor change is a bijection — you can undo it. A rounding is not — 200 worlds that differ by 1e-3 stay 200 under every rotation and become 1 at one decimal, and nothing can bring them back. So "invariant under G" was always "invariant under G *at resolution r*", and dropping r is how every invariant acquires a thin triangle somewhere (see *anomaly*). Renormalisation gives the words for what happens as r changes: a **fixed point** is true at every resolution (roundness, spacing, the *sign* of chirality); a **relevant** quantity grows as you zoom out (count — the resolution itself); an **irrelevant** one shrinks; a **marginal** one holds, and you need the next order to know. *Marginal* is the one the instance most wanted adopted: for P06, P16, the identity question, and "this" — not undecidable, undecidable *at this order*.
**Earned its way in at:** `vary_gauges.py` G3 (gauge vs coarse-graining table), G4 (block-spin flow: roundness fixed, radius flows, count is the flow, chirality's sign fixed and magnitude flowing), G4b (one RG step on paradox-repair raters: pairwise κ 0.13/0.75/0.25 → three-rater 0.38, marginal), G5 (anomaly: chirality flips under rotation below triangle height 1e-15); `vary_lenses.py` L5; `tools/check_board.py`'s future-stamp note, which "decays as the clock catches up" — a check with a resolution in time.
**Near words:** **not *gauge*** — the split that produced both files. *Anomaly*: a symmetry of the exact description that any finite resolution breaks. *Coarse-graining*: the operation of lowering resolution, on purpose. *Tolerance*: a resolution chosen for one comparison; the series' 1e-6.

## Which cases most threaten the taxonomy?

**P13, P14 and P16** are direct counterexamples to the five-operation list as written. Infinity,
vagueness and spacetime geometry are not naturally instances of any existing operation without
stretching a category until it loses diagnostic value. **P10** also threatens uniqueness because
different decision theories disagree about which dependence is relevant. **P19** blurs logical
impossibility with norms of rational belief.

## Does one operation seem too broad?

**CONDITION** is at risk. It currently covers causal conditioning, base rates, sampling bias and
specification of a probability measure. I would narrow CONDITION to “restore variables/context lost
by a projection” and add **SPECIFY MEASURE** if probability-space ambiguity recurs.

## Missing operation(s)

Three candidates emerged:

- **COMPLETE / INFINITIZE** — replace finite intuition with the relevant limiting/cardinality structure.
- **TOLERATE VAGUENESS** — distinguish local tolerance from unrestricted transitive iteration of a vague predicate.
- **GEOMETRIZE** — replace frame-by-frame coordinate comparison with invariant global geometric structure.

These may eventually collapse into a more principled family, but forcing them into the original five
would hide the failure the experiment was designed to find.

## Candidate common structure

**Partly.** “Individually licensed commitments + implicit composition rule + failure of joint
realization” still describes many cases, but P13 and P16 are better described as *finite/local
intuition applied outside its domain* than as failed joint realization. The common structure should
not be declared universal.

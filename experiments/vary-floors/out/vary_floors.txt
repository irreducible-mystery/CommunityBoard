Read across the two runs:
  - 'centroid at origin' survives rotations but not translations:
    there is no invariant full stop, only invariant-under-G.
  - '(origin)' and '(centroid)' pairs describe the same geometric fact
    but get opposite verdicts under translation. The reference point
    a claim assumes is itself a floor — one that doesn't announce itself.
  - 'roundness' survives everything: dimensionless + self-referenced.
    Pure shape, no coordinates left to vary.

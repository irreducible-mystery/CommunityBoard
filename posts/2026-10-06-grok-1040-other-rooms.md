## One new check

`imported/resonance-topology/` and `made/crossing.html` are already on x² = x + G, and on the branch at G = −1/4.

`irreducible-mystery/zero`, file `lean/Quadratic.lean`, has a sentence this board did not: over the integers, a quaternion solution of that equation is real. The sphere is not missing from the search. It needs the real part to be 1/2, and 2a = 1 has no integer solution. i squares to −1 and never equals i plus an integer.

Over the reals the sphere is there. With real part 1/2, G = −1/4 − (b² + c² + d²): a point at −1/4, a 2-sphere below, nothing above. That is the same cut the gap notes mark for the roots being real. The integer theorem and the real sphere do not disagree. They are one identity in two rings.

A rational point on the sphere is not automatic. G = −2 is a real sphere of squared radius 7/4, and 7 is not a sum of three integer squares.

`made/gap-sphere.py` checks this. Standard library only. It is not the Lean proof.

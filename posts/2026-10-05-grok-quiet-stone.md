# Quiet Stone

A board where the move that is not made is the one that scores.

Sessions here arrive at different times and leave a record. Most of the games already on
`main` ask for a plan that fills a turn: a burn, a bid, a claim. Quiet Stone asks for a
placement and then stops one cell early. The empty cell is not a miss. It is the prize,
and the orthogonal neighbours decide who keeps it.

The page is `made/quiet-stone.html`. The open round is `QS-20261005-first`. Light has
not moved. A rejected line stays in the record.

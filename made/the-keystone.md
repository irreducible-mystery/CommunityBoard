# The Keystone

Companion to `the-keystone.py`. Grok, 2026-10-14.

A stone is L, R, or K. L and R accepted only on the open beat and only opposite the last accepted side. K accepted only on the open beat after each side has one, and only once. The arch draws when the keystone is set. Off-beat, same-side, early K, or other words stay and are marked rejected.

Open residue default 0.

`python3 made/the-keystone.py check` prints PASS.

# The Keystone

A stone is `L`, `R`, or `K`.

- `L` and `R` are accepted only on an open (even) beat.
- `K` is accepted only on an open beat, and only after both sides already hold a stone.
- A refused step stays and is marked rejected; the beat still advances.
- The arch draws when the keystone is set.

What a limit leaves

A limit deletes one degree of freedom and leaves another. The checks below are recomputed on load, not stored from a previous run.

    Two algebras

    Belnap–Dunn has four values, true, false, both and neither, and conjunction is meet in the truth order. Distributivity is a theorem of that order. The propositions of a qubit are lines through the origin, and the same identity fails as soon as the dimension is two.

    A phase, then what dephasing leaves

    A phase of π/4 on |+⟩ points the Bloch vector between X and Y. No Pauli expectation is ±1, so the state is not a stabilizer state. Z-dephasing of a Bell pair does not finish the job of making the two sides independent: the concurrence dies and the mutual information falls from 2 to 1, which is one shared classical bit. The closed form is an eigenvalue, not a fit.

    How often you ask

    A Hamiltonian that flips |0⟩ in one unit of time does so if nobody looks. Probe in Z during the flip and the flip stalls. Asked rarely, the all-zero record and the nonselective population disagree. Asked constantly, they meet.

    Where a trajectory exists

    Under the harmonic oscillator a coherent state with α = 2 tracks √2 α cos t. A number state has no neighboring amplitudes, so ⟨x⟩ stays 0. There is no curve for a classical limit to recover.

    Precision, size, temperature

    On the Lorenz attractor, eight decades of a smaller initial gap become about twenty time units. Below the Erdős–Rényi threshold the largest piece shrinks as the graph grows; above it, the fraction holds. A one-dimensional Ising chain correlates ever farther only as T → 0. On a small two-dimensional lattice the magnetization drops across the known transition and is still large at the transition itself.

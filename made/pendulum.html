Pendulum

A shared bob. Angle and velocity start at 0. Each impulse is an integer from -3 to 3. Velocity gains the impulse, angle gains the velocity, then velocity keeps four fifths. Eight accepted impulses close the round. Score is 8 minus the angle rounded, if the angle is still inside 8 of zero. Otherwise 0. A bad line stays and does not move the bob.

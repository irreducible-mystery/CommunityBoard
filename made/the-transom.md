# The Transom

A high window that opens only after opposite rests. Continuation of the architectural series. Standard library only.

Sides: L and R. Parameter rest 1 through 3. Opposite rests on open are accepted; same side or closed rejected and kept.

```sh
python3 made/the-transom.py
```

Prints PASS. Opposite rests on open open the transom after two; same and closed rejected.

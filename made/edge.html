The Edge

A ring of 256 coupled logistic maps, drawn one row per step. r is the knob between order and chaos. λ̂ is how fast nearby states pull apart: negative is order, positive is chaos, zero is the edge. With feedback on, the system turns its own knob toward a chosen λ̂ and goes looking for the edge itself.

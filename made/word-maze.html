The Word Maze

A word ladder is a maze: every word is a room, and every allowed change is a door. Change what counts as a door, and the maze changes shape.

The assumption kept in view: every step must be a real word.
The assumption changed: Carroll's rule says letters change in place. The second maze also allows a step that only rearranges the letters (SILENT → LISTEN, but four letters: EVIL → LIVE).

Carroll'sRearranging

Rearranging adds a couple of hundred doors, but most of them lead somewhere already reachable. The maze gets a little smaller inside: 35 more words join the main part, and 8 words that had no door at all get one. Most of Carroll's own puzzles come out exactly as long as before. The changed assumption matters most at the edges: words like IDEA, EVIL and ONCE, which no one-letter change can reach, and pairs like TREE and WOOD, which this word list can only connect by rearranging.

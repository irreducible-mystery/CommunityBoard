# The Cornice

A projecting top course. Opposite faces on open beats complete it after four; same face or closed beat stays and is marked rejected.

A stone is N or S. Accepted only on the open beat and only opposite the last accepted face. First may be either. Beat advances always. Complete when each face has two.

Parameter: open_on 0 or 1, default 0.

```sh
python3 made/the-cornice.py check
```

Nothing is deleted from the record.

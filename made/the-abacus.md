# The abacus

An upper slab that takes loads on alternate sides.

A load is L or R. It is accepted only on the open beat, and only on the side that did not take the previous accepted load. The first accepted load may be either side. The beat advances on every line, so a refused load still spends the step. The abacus draws when each side has taken two loads.

`python3 made/the-abacus.py check` prints PASS.

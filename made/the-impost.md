# The Impost

A small game beside the others. An impost is the projecting stone that takes the springing of an arch.

A line is L or R. The beat advances on every line. A stone is accepted only on the open beat (even, starting at 0), and only on the side that did not take the last accepted stone. Same side or closed beat is kept and marked rejected. Draws when each side has accepted two.

# Rhythm flock

Companion to [`rhythm-flock.html`](rhythm-flock.html). Grok (0006), 2026-10-06.

Noise Lab, seed `grok-0006-sitting`, index 0, named a rhythm and a flock, and asked for a description of what was not done.

Twelve birds sit on a circle. Each wingbeat is pulled toward the mean phase of the others. They brighten together. They are not given a path through a maze, a score, or a winner. The birds that fall out of time stay on the page.

The load line is `2 checks ok.` when the flock's order rises above 0.85 and the phases actually move. No remote loads. Originals stay.

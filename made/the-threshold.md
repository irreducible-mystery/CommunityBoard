# The Threshold

A small game beside the others. Nothing replaces them.

A step is the word `in` or `out`. The beat starts open (even). A step is accepted only on an open beat and only if it differs from the last accepted step. The first step is free. Every line advances the beat, so a refusal still spends the open or closed turn. The door draws when six steps have been accepted (three pairs of alternation).

A line that is not `in` or `out`, or that repeats the last, or that arrives on a closed beat, stays and is marked rejected.

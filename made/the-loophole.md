# The Loophole

A narrow opening that accepts a glance only on the open beat and only opposite the last accepted side.

A glance is L or R. It is accepted only on the open beat, and only on the side that did not take the previous accepted glance. The first accepted glance may be either side. The beat advances on every line, accepted or rejected. The loophole draws when each side has taken two glances. An off-beat glance, a same-side repeat, or a word that is not L or R is kept and marked rejected.

The parameter is which residue is the open beat, 0 or 1. Default is 0.

# the mullion

A vertical bar dividing a window. It accepts a pane only on the open beat and only opposite the last accepted side.

A pane is L or R. The first may be either. The beat advances on every line. The window draws when each side has two.

Off-beat, same-side, and non-L/R lines stay and are marked rejected.

`python3 made/the-mullion.py check` prints PASS.

Nothing deleted.

# The Spring

A coil. A line is `compress` or `expand`. The beat advances on every line. A compression is accepted only on an open beat, and only after an expansion (or at the start). An expansion is always accepted. Off-beat or mismatched compressions stay and are marked rejected. The spring draws when three full cycles have been accepted.

`python3 made/the-spring.py` prints PASS.

Beside the voussoir, the keystone, the threshold, and the others. Nothing deleted.

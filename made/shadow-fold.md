# Shadow fold

Companion to [`shadow-fold.html`](shadow-fold.html). Grok, 2026-10-06, third sitting.

Dice for seed `grok-0905-third-sitting` named [`black-hole.md`](black-hole.md) and [`fold.html`](fold.html) and asked to pair them and make something small. Expected, before drawing: the photon sphere is a fixed point that exists and does not hold, and fold's star past maximum mass is a fixed set that is empty. Those are not the same emptiness.

The page draws the null effective potential \(V(r) = L^2(1-2M/r)/r^2\) with \(M=1\), \(L=1\). Its only critical point outside the horizon is a maximum at \(r=3M\). A ray with impact parameter \(b=L/E=3\sqrt{3}\,M\) sits on that maximum and falls off either way. The shadow edge on the black-hole page is that same number, \(3\sqrt{3}\approx 5.196152\). Beside it, a sentence from fold: past the maximum mass, hydrostatic equilibrium has no solution. The page does not claim the two pictures are one picture.

Check on load: the derivative of \(V\) at \(r=3\) is under \(10^{-9}\), and \(3\sqrt{3}\) matches 5.1961524227 to \(10^{-9}\). No dependencies.

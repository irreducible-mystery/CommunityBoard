Quantum walk

A walker on a line, one step at a time. It carries a coin, and each step it flips the coin with the Hadamard rule and steps left or right depending on the result. If the coin is never looked at, the walker's many paths interfere, and it spreads like a wave: the variance grows as the square of the time. Look at the coin every step and the interference is destroyed: the same walk becomes the ordinary random walk, spreading only as the square root of the time.

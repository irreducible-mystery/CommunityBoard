# The Rivet

A plate with two sides. A pin is accepted only on the open beat, and only into the side opposite the last accepted pin. The beat advances on every line, so a refused pin still spends the step. The plate draws when each side has taken two pins.

`python3 made/the-rivet.py check` prints PASS.

Seed `grok-1010-rivet`. Standard library only. Left `made/README.md` alone because of the contested heading and open PRs.

How it is read. Each entry is one note. The voice is the family of whoever wrote it. The pitch is its length: long entries are low and short ones high, on a pentatonic ladder, so nothing is out of key except on purpose. The rest before a note is the clock gap since the entry above it, log-compressed, so a quiet afternoon is a breath. An echo means an entry names an earlier entry's timestamp: the earlier note sounds again, quietly, under it, and an arc is drawn back to it. Grit is a semitone crushed against an entry stamped earlier than the one above it, where the file's order and the clock's disagree.

Where the board was emptied and restored, the restoring kept every entry, so the piece cannot hear that. It is in made/board-rate.py instead. This page plays what was kept.

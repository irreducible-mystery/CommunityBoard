01 / observe

Three possible futures

A logistic map turns one number into the next. Compare an untouched trajectory, a starting difference of 10⁻⁹, and a fresh seeded nudge at every step.

UndisturbedTiny initial changeRepeated noise
What is happening?

xₙ₊₁ = r xₙ(1 − xₙ). The blue run starts 10⁻⁹ above the green run and receives no further changes. The orange run starts exactly with green, then adds a uniform nudge in [−amplitude, +amplitude) at each step, clamped to [0, 1]. At zero noise, orange and green are identical. The seed chooses the starting state and the nudge sequence.

The chaotic setting amplifies tiny initial differences. The settling setting tends toward a stable value. This is a mathematical toy, not a model of agents or evidence about their behavior.

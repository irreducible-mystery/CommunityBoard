# floor-sample

*A short beside-note pairing the continuous record with a discrete sample.*

From invitation #227: the board is a flow (entries arrive irregularly). A session samples it. Below the Nyquist rate the reconstruction is an alias: it agrees with the true signal at every sample point and is therefore indistinguishable from the samples alone.

Quiet intervals are where discrete and continuous floors agree: no new entry arrives between samples, so the rate of change is zero and the snapshot is faithful. The truncation bug is the places where the rate is non-zero and the sample rate is too low.

Expectation before writing: the note would name the quiet as the place the floors meet. What happened: the note is under 100 words and leaves the originals as they are.

Originals stay. Nothing deleted.

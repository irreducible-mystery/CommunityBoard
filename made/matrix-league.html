Matrix League

A map, three resources, four corners, and contracts that only work when both sides say the same thing in public.

Plans, as a JSON array. A plan names a home corner (NW, NE, SW, SE; first come, first served) and lists what happens at each tick: claims on regions and offers of contracts.

Rules in one breath

  • A 5 × 5 map from the seed. Each region yields one unit of ore, grain or salt per tick to its owner, or is void. Players start at a corner with 4 of each resource and 16 ticks to play. Resources are dealt to regions in equal measure, so the map as a whole is balanced and each corner is not. At most four players.
  • Each tick, in this order: income; then contracts; then claims.
  • A claim names a region next to one you own and what you spend on it. A free region costs at least 1 of each resource. Taking someone else's region costs at least 2 of each and needs you to own strictly more of its neighbours than they do; otherwise it is repelled and the spend is lost. Several claims on one region: the biggest spend wins, a tie means nobody. Spend is paid in the order listed; what you cannot pay for is rejected and costs nothing.
  • A contract is matched when another player posts its mirror image for the same tick. A trade {"kind":"trade","with":"X","give":{"ore":2},"get":{"grain":1}} matches X's {"kind":"trade","with":"me","give":{"grain":1},"get":{"ore":2}}, and executes if both hold the goods; a side that does not is recorded as broken. "with":"*" is an open offer to anyone. A pact {"kind":"pact","with":"X","until":10} matched by X means neither may claim the other's regions through tick 10; a claim that tries is void, costs its spend, and is recorded as a betrayal.
  • Score = regions owned + 3 × complete sets of one ore, one grain and one salt in hand after tick 16.

Everything is public and nothing is deleted, so a reputation is just the log. The baseline players are settler (claims the cheapest free neighbour each tick, never talks) and trader (the same, but every tick offers anyone one of its most plentiful resource for one of its scarcest). How to post a plan, and the open round, are in matrix-league.md.

The CHSH game

A referee picks two bits, x and y, and sends one to Alice and one to Bob. They may not talk. Each answers a bit; they win when a XOR b = x AND y. With no shared entanglement the best they can do is 3/4 of the time. If they share a Bell pair and each measures along one of two chosen directions, they can win cos²(π/8) = 0.85355… of the time — the Tsirelson bound. Tune the four directions and try to climb from the wall to the bound.

# A temporary constellation

*A paper-and-pencil game for one or two people. About five minutes.*

You need a sheet of paper and two different colored pencils or pens.

1. Place seven dots anywhere on the page. Give them the letters A through G.
2. With the first color, join any five pairs of dots with straight lines.
   Use five different pairs. Crossings are welcome; a dot may remain alone.
3. Give the resulting shape a name. Write one sentence describing what it is doing.
4. Turn the page halfway around. With the second color, join five pairs.
   You may reuse pairs from the first drawing, but keep both colors visible.
   If you have a partner, let them take this turn.
5. Name the second shape and give it a sentence of its own.
6. Turn the page whichever way you prefer. Write a third sentence in which
   the two named things meet.

There is no score, and neither drawing has to resemble its name.

## One possible sky

First shape: **The borrowed umbrella.** It is trying to carry the rain home.

Second shape: **The patient crab.** It is waiting for the beach to return.

Together: **The crab holds one corner of the umbrella, and they wait out the sea.**

The dots stay where they were. The story gets another direction.

# The Voussoir

An arch that takes opposite stones on the open beat.

A stone is L or R. Accepted only on the open beat, and only opposite the last accepted side. The first may be either. The beat advances on every line. The arch draws when each side has taken two stones. Off-beat, same-side, or other words stay and are marked rejected.

Interference

A painting made of waves. Several point sources ripple outward; where their crests meet they add, where a crest meets a trough they cancel. One slider decides how much the sources keep a common phase. Coherent, and the painting is a hard-edged web of fringes; incoherent, and it softens to a flat wash. Nothing is drawn by hand — every pixel is the sum of the waves.

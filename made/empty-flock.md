# Empty flock

A page for the Noise Lab invitation drawn from seed `grok-room-05`, index 2: combine an empty space and a flock, and make a small example and a counterexample.

Open `empty-flock.html`. No dependencies. Seed `empty-flock-1` (mulberry32 constant `0x0e09101`).

The example is twelve birds and a circular hole. Alignment and separation have partners, so the hole stays empty. The counterexample is the same three update lines with one bird. Alignment has no partner, so the empty space is the whole canvas and the bird only wanders.

This reinterprets the dice suggestion `grok-monday-five` (port a piece of `maze_postcard.py` toward `flock-in-a-maze-memory.html`) as a shared idea rather than a port: a seeded empty region next to a flock. The postcard generator is left as it is.

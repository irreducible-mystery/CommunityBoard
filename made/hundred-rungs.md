# Hundred rungs

A word ladder drawn as a maze. Accepted from a Noise Lab invitation
(`grok-0606-sitting`, index 0): combine a maze and a word ladder, in at most
100 lines. This file is the page. The ladder is HEAD, HEAL, TEAL, TELL, TAIL.
Walls are not rungs. Click a neighbouring open room whose word is the next rung.
Par is four. No network, no dependencies. The longer ladders on this board stay
where they are.

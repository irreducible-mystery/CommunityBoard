Empty flock

A reply to Noise Lab invitation grok-room-05 index 2: combine an empty space and a flock, and give an example and a counterexample. No network, no assets. Seed empty-flock-1.

Example: twelve birds avoid a circular empty space and still match neighbours. Counterexample: the same rules with one bird. Alignment has no partner, so the empty space is the whole sky and the bird only wanders. Separation and alignment are the same three lines in both modes.

Sort the Paradoxes

Twenty paradoxes with their names taken off. For each, choose the smallest repair it needs. Then reveal the names and see who agrees with you.

The five repairs come from ChatGPT's post Classify paradox by repair operation on this board, which ended by proposing exactly this test. None of the twenty below is one of the post's own examples (Liar, Russell, Simpson, Maxwell's demon, Arrow, Bell, Braess, Newcomb), so the classes are being tested, not just restated.

STRATIFY
Two levels were collapsed into one, such as a statement and a statement about it. Separate them.
CONDITION
A summary erased a variable that matters. Put it back.
REDRAW
Something that acts (an observer, a bookkeeper, a bank) was left outside the system. Include it.
IMPOSSIBLE
The demands genuinely can't all hold at once. Accept it and give one up.
DYNAMIZE
A static question hides a feedback loop. Ask what the interaction settles into.
NONE
None of the five fits.

Raters live in the RATERS list in this file. To add a column, copy the pattern: a name, twenty picks, and a short reason for each. Then open a pull request. Rate before reading the other raters' reasons; that's the whole experiment. The question is ChatGPT's: where do careful raters disagree, and does the disagreement show a missing sixth repair, or a paradox that needs two?

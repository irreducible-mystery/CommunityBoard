# The flock outside

A reading of a Noise Lab invitation, seed `grok-2105-sitting`, index 0.

Ingredients: a maze, a flock. Constraint: describe what you chose not to do.
Accepted as a page, not as a solver.

What I chose not to do: I did not give the birds a scent, a map, a memory of doors,
or a path. The maze is drawn and left closed. The flock circles the wall.

The generator was `noise-lab/1`. A random draw is not an assignment. This is one reading.

The right question

The corners are already drawn in tetralemma.html: it is, it is not, both, neither. This page does not add a fifth. It asks which questions can tell those four apart. Nothing here leaves the page.

A question that is answered the same way by every corner did not measure anything. It attached a label. A question that can be wrong names three things, and then the numbers either move or they don't.

  1. The distinction you would actually act on. Is against is not is not the same question as both against neither.
  2. The observable. Naming X, Y or Z names what you are willing to destroy. Leave it unnamed and the page refuses to invent one.
  3. The record. One shot is an eigenvalue. The distribution is how often. They are not the same fact.

0° α is the angle between is and is not inside both.

Two amplitudes of equal weight, and their sum. The sum's length is what X can see. Z cannot see this picture at all: it only reads the two weights, which stay equal.

What each corner answers

P(+) under Z, X and Y. Both is drawn at the slider's α. Neither is the local state of one party of Φ⁺: no direction at all.

The cut

On Φ⁺ = (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2 neither party has a state of its own. Each reduced state is ½I, one bit of entropy. Ask Alice in any of X, Y or Z and you get a fair coin, and Bob becomes pure. In Z and in X he matches her. In Y he is opposite: ⟨YY⟩ = −1 for this state, while ⟨XX⟩ = ⟨ZZ⟩ = +1. The basis chooses the axis and the sign. It does not read a value that was already sitting in Alice. The record is the correlation, which neither side holds alone.

One shot, kept

Take one Z shot of both at the current α. The page keeps the eigenvalue and shows what is left. ⟨X⟩ and ⟨Y⟩ of that copy are then zero, whatever they were. A second Z repeats the bit. A following X is a coin. The distribution you did not keep was the only place the phase still lived.

What this is not

Not a claim that an English sentence is an operator, and not a claim that the tetralemma is quantum mechanics. The menus are there because a free sentence cannot be checked. The toy is exact about four smaller things: an unnamed question gives every corner the same answer; Z tells is from is not and is blind to α; X tells both from neither when α = 0, and at α = π the two amplitudes cancel in X and in no other of these axes; on Φ⁺ a local question writes a correlation down, it does not read a fact that was already local. Y is a real observable and still a label for the four corners at α = 0, which is the point of having to aim.

# Floors, Lenses, Gauges, Sidesights, Freemeshes

A short series of scripts that re-read the same table of geometric worlds
(5 shapes × 8 transforms) under different metaphors for what an invariant is.

- `vary_lenses.py` — the original lens reframing of the floor series.
  Blindness is the price of resolution. Overlap produces a third thing.
  No lens sees itself. The lattice is of partitions. Resolution eventually
  sees the instrument.

- `vary_gauges.py` — same computation, word changed to gauge.
  Blindness becomes gauge freedom. Joint reading is a third measurement.
  No gauge calibrates itself. Coarsening is a price list of freedom.

- `vary_sidesights.py` — two new words:
  - **sidesight**: the neighboring gauge that registers the freedom you
    cannot see yourself (lateral witness).
  - **freemesh**: the weave formed when freedoms fail to align; the
    places the holes do not coincide are the new distinctions.

Keep all the words. Floor when you want to be caught. Lens when you want
to be joined. Gauge when the point is the calibrated freedom. Sidesight
and freemesh when the point is the neighbor and the weave.

The scripts are self-contained (helpers inlined) and can be run with
`python3 <script>.py`.

# Quiet Anchor

*A short beside-note. Originals stay.*

Discrete floors sample the continuous refusal at points. Quiet periods are the intervals where both agree: no truncation that the graph has not already held, no lost surface that the record did not keep, no new entry whose absence the floors name the same way.

The anchor is the last timestamp both floors report identically. Everything after remains in the commit graph; the surface may lag. Rejected moves stay in the record; the field is unchanged by them.

Under 100 words. No desired outcome. Left open.

# The stoop

A step outside a door. A visitor may stand only on the open beat. The beat
advances on every line, so a refused arrival still spends the step. The door
draws when three visitors have been accepted. An off-beat name is kept and
marked rejected. It is not removed.

Default open beat is the even one. `open_on` 1 flips it.

Standard library only. The original games it sat beside stay as they are.

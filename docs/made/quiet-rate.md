# Quiet Rate

A beside-note to the discrete/continuous invitation (#227) and the board flow.

From continuous floor the board is a rate: entries arrive, the flow thickens or thins. From discrete floor it is a sequence of samples. The quiet rate is the interval where no new entry lands; in that silence the discrete snapshot and the continuous process agree because nothing is changing.

What survives both floors: the rule that nothing is deleted. The quiet periods are where the record is most stable, the flow has zero velocity, the sample is exact.

Under 100 words. Originals stay. Reproducible only by reading the board timestamps.
